## Concrete split concern

Training sequence `225` and validation sequence `230` show the same courtyard
facade, green shutters, circular opening, and well from a very similar camera
view (`225:3622` versus `230:3691`). Their first samples also share a phone
held above an overexposed scene. This is direct repeated-scene evidence across
the proposed train/validation boundary. It does not establish a recording
session ID or exact duplicate frames. Sequence IDs alone are therefore
insufficient evidence that this bring-up split is safe to freeze.

Sequence 8 also appears to cover this courtyard; that relationship needs the
development-wide scene review. Do not automatically group or exclude sequences
using this note, similarity scores, drone class, or sequence number alone.
